# Christmas Dinner

A phone web app for Billy, Max and George to take Christmas dinner plate orders. Open it in the browser, or add it to the home screen. It works offline once it has loaded, and it keeps the orders on that phone.

**Live:** https://ashridge-cpu.github.io/christmas-dinner/

## How to use

1. Swipe, or use the arrows, to pick a person.
2. Tap a food on the left to put it on the plate (Medium to start with).
3. Tap the food again, or tap it on the plate, to choose Small, Medium or Large, or to take it off.
4. Press **Order** to save that plate and move on.
5. Press **List** for the kitchen sheet. You can print it, share it, or save it as a text file.

Settings (the cog at the top) lets you add or rename a diner, and start again.

## Add to the home screen on Android

1. Open the link in Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
4. Tap **Add**.

## Notes

- Orders are stored only on the phone you are using. Pass one phone around the table so everyone is on the same list.
- Printing needs a printer the phone can reach, or use **Save as PDF** in the print screen. If you cannot print, use **Share / Save the list**.
- There are no accounts and no server. Nothing is sent anywhere.
